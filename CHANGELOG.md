# Junk Drop 1.3.0

- Your own **junk lists**: one for this character and one for all your characters. Items on
  either count as junk, whatever their color, and skip the silver cap.
- A list editor in `/dui` > Plugins > Junk Drop: the white items in your bags, cheapest first,
  each with Add, For all and Ignore. Drop any other item in the box or type its name. Both lists
  show icons and sell prices, with Share / Only here to move an item between them and Remove to
  take it off.
- An **Ignored** list (shared by all characters): ignored items are left out of the bag list and
  never deleted, even gray ones. On a wide options window it sits beside the bag list.
- The bag list skips items with no sell price, like the Hearthstone.
- Chat commands too: `/dropjunk add`, `addall`, `ignore`, `remove`, `list`, `clear`,
  `clearall` and `clearignored`. `/dropjunk help` lists them.
